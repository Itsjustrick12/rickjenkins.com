#  My Favorite Monkey:

![Monkey Profile Picture](/blog/FirstPost/monkey.png)

This is a picture of a Golden snub-nosed monkey. He has often been my profile picture on several social platforms like discord and github. If I used instagram or other socials he'd probably be my picture there too.

I've consistently mistakenly called these guys gibbons over the years, but it turns out they are just a monkeys. You learn something new every day.

## The Golden Snub-Nosed Monkey

Today I spent time learning about my favorite monkey. 

Coolest Facts:
* They are some of the only primates in the world to have naturally blue faces!
* They have furry tails that are thick enough for them to use as seat cushions. Weird thing, makes me thing of Kingambit the Pokemon.
* They are able to survival in the cold, high mountains of China. They brave the cold by huddling closely in "giant" groups to share body heat.

I have a bucket list item that says I have to go to china and see one in person. Check out [this video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UkCbehpGBME) to see him in action. He's a grubby little guy, and I really like him. Like specifically this one. The other ones aren't nearly as fat and greedy as this specific guy. He doesn't even say thank you.
