On Saturday, November 14th, our highschool friend group took a day trip out to go to the Dupont Circle Holiday Market and the National Postal Museum. The trip was meant to serve as the big hangout for Sofie's 22nd birthday the day before.

It was fun to see everyone put some effort into their cold day outfits. My favorite bit from the day was trying to compliment randon strangers on their outfits nonchalantly. We quickly forgot the bit after leaving the holiday market, but its important to note that people were truly fitted up on November, 14th 2026.

The postal museum is an actually pretty interesting museum despite what I imagine to be a relatively uninteresting topic to most. We enjoyed looking at all the cool stamp designs and learning about the cool technology used to transport all kinds of information and items via mail.
