## An Incredible Kickoff to a Month of Travel

Five days after graduating from UMBC, I hopped on a non-stop United flight to Tokyo, Japan. This flight marked the start of an entire month of travel in Japan, meant to be a graudation gift to myself, and an opportunity to use the Japanese language skills I spent the past two semesters building.

I spent the first week of the trip with my younger sister Jamie. We spent our timing exploring all kinds of different areas across the Kantō region. It was Jamie's first time out of the United States, so I made sure to curate a one week itinerary that involved Tokyo as well as beautiful nature-y areas I knew she'd like. We ended up hitting Tokyo, Odawara, Atami, Kamakura, Enoshima, and Yokohama bouncing around with the wonderful Japanese public transport systems. 

We had an absolute blast together. From our first independent onsen experiences, trying amazake for the first time (its SO good), hiking to the tallest point in Hakone, and many other experiences, we did our best to put ourselves out there, trying new things every oppurtunity we got. We may have been burned by that disgusting jelly thing at the teahouse and realizing we aren't city people in Yokohama, but we always found a way to quickly move on and keep exploring.

Overall, my trip with Jamie completely MOGGED my trip with Will several years back. The cool May weather felt like heaven compared to the sweltering heat Will and I dealt with in August of 2023. Regardless, I can't wait to go back with *both* my siblings sometime in the distant future. I hope you enjoy the pictures!
