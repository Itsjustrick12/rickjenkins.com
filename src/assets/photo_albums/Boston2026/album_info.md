The photos you see below are from Sofie and I's 6 day road trip up into the New England region of the United States. We originally planned the trip around just visiting Boston, but it grew in scale to making our way up further North to Vermont so I could add a new state to my list. It also happened to line up perfectly with my brother being in New Hampshire for his best friend Eric's wedding, which gave us the chance to visit Eric and his now-wife, and also cart Will up to get Vermont on his list, too!

We started off with some Rise Up Coffee and immediately got stuck on the Eastern Shore of Maryland because I ignored the directions. We stopped to get some Jersey Mike's in New Jersey after about 4 hours, then we stopped for Indian Food in Worcester, MA, where we ate indian food because we were STARVING after another 4-5 ish hours. After struggling to find a good parking solution near our Airbnb, we called it a day.

The next morning we started with some breakfast near our Airbnb in Cambridge, then walked all the way past MIT and across the Charles River to take a look at some shops, looking for trinkets. We then made our way to the beautiful Boston Common park, which I had been to before in the dead of Winter. In the Fall, it's an amazingly serene place as long as there aren't too many people around. We peopled watched for a while before walking over to the Faneuil Hall food market, where we tried a really expensive seafood sampler that had some lobster, crab, and shrimp in little rolls. From there we did a silly move hopping on a Ferry for one stop to go to a event brite type pumpkin patch thing in Seaport that we looked at for three seconds and walked past. We did get Van Luuween though, so it wasn't a total waste of time. After that, we found our way to Brookline where we had spiked Baja Blasts and played 2 games of Wingspan at the local board game cafe, Knight Moves. Brilliant name for that business, by the way. After an Uber fumble, we found ourselves back in Seaport, NOT Cambridge, at some random noodle place and made the arduous journey back to Cambridge where I nearly shit myself getting scared by people walking too fast and homeless people trailing us. Anyways, that was Day one!

The next day we walked around Harvard, having breakfast at a fun restaurant down the street from the campus. It felt pretty cool to be at the site of a Channel 5 video I watched like a year ago and going "woah! I recognize that arch!" silly stuff like that. From there we found ourselves near Fenway Park, joking about the Ruggles station name and walking through yet another pretty park. After spontaneously stopping to watch a D3 soccer game, we did a little walk through Beacon Hill. After that, we found ourselves at god's gift to Boston, Regina Pizzeria. A legendary spot that Will and I visited a couple of years ago. After like a 40 minute wait, we ate two slices and got full. But! I got a sick ass Regina 100 year anniversary hat. You'll see me wearing it for the rest of the trip. Finally, to round out the day, and our time in Boston, we went to the Medium Build concert at the Royale. If you haven't had a chance to read my blog post about [The King of Having Fun](https://rickjenkins.com/#/blog/KingOfHavingFun), go do that, it talks more about our amazing experience at the concert and how I found Medium Build's music. It was a great way to end our time in the City.

We left Boston early in the morning to go scoop my older brother Will from a nearby city where he was staying with a friend. We grabbed him, ate some breakfast, then started our journey up to Vermont. Spontaneously, we decided to visit Eric in Manchester, New Hampshire, where he and his soon to be wife, Corinne, lived. We walked around the sleepy city, grabbed coffee, and talked by the nearby river. It was fun getting to meet Eric's fiancée and talking with them about their careers and experiences moving in together. I had met Eric before, but it was my first time meeting Corinne, who is very kind and wonderful to talk to.

Leaving Manchester, we finally got on our way up towards Vermont's capital city, Burlington. The foliage was GORGEOUS, and it was so nice that the somewhat overcast and rainy weather didn't take too much away from the scenery. Will and I caught up a good bit, we stopped at a McDonald's, and then we found ourselves hopping out of the car several times to take in the foliage and just be happy to be outside with the beautiful trees. While we didn't do the lame move of visiting Noah Kahan's hometown of Strafford, Vermont, we did make sure to bump the Great Divide as we got closer to Burlington. It was so perfect, and it made me appricieate the music in that amazing album just a little bit better than I already did.

We ended up stopping in Waterbury, as we got super hungry. We walked around the small town in the darkness waiting for a table to open up at the local BBQ spot. It was funny goofing around with Will and Sofie together. We had a great meal at Prohibition Pig, then made the final leg to our separate accommodations in Burlington.

The next day, Sofie and I left Will to spend the day out in beautiful ski town, Stowe. It's supposedly pretty touristy, but that didn't take away from the fact that it was downright GOURGEOUS with the Fall foliage. We ate a a cool ski cabin breakfast spot, then raced over to take a gondola ride up Mount Mansfield. It was Sofie's first time on a gondola, and our first time witnessing what could possibly be the most beautiful landscape we've ever seen. Just look at the pictures! It was easily the peak of our trip, it was just so breathtaking after a couple of days of rain and half-perfect pictures. Looking at it again, the pictures I took aren't really all that. However, believe me when I say it was an absolutely unforgettable sight seeing the sun break through the clouds for the first time during our trip. On the mountain, Sofie and I made a pretty silly decision to hike the "Challenging" route to the peak of Mount Mansfield, which we quit 80% of the way through due to a crazy vertical climb section near the top. Still in Stowe, we stopped by a local maple shop, then went to an apple orchard store, where we tried apple cider and dinky little donuts. I picked up some Ciders for PJ (that I still haven't given to him!), and then we got back on the road again. On the way back to Burlington, we checked out the Ben and Jerry's factory, but didn't go in or bother spending $10 on a single cone.

We made it to downtown Burlington in the early evening to meet back up with Will. We walked along Lake Champlain, hearing about Will's solo day and talking about all kinds of things. Burlington unfortunately features a massive homeless population that honestly really impacted the charm of the city. We dodged organized hordes and found our way to a Ben & Jerry's before eating a nice burger at a local restaurant. After all that, we said goodbye to Will, and Sofie and I crashed back at the hotel. The next day we made the massive journey back to the Annapolis area, braving heavy rainstorms and the hellhole that is New Jersey to finally get home.

This might have been the first time I understood the whole "It's about the journey, not the destination" sentiment. Just driving in my brand new Corolla with my lovely girlfriend and brother was such an amazing experience I would kill to relive. Thankfully, we've got these pictures we can revisit! Hope you enjoy looking at them!
